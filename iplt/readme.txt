Steps to install Plt on your iPhone:

(1) Download the distribution file from
http://audres.org/downloads/plt-ios.zip.

(2) Drag the files contained in plt-ios.zip into Apps section of
iTunes and check that the Plt icon appears as one of the Apps.

(3) Connect your iPhone and drag the Plt icon from the Apps section
to your Device and Sync.

After it's installed, you should be able to open pltfiles that are
attached to mail messages.
